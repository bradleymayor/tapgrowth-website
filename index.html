Information

Common Questions.

Do customers need an app?

No. Compatible phones can read the NFC stand or keychain directly.

Do I need to program anything?

No. TapGrowth prepares the NFC destination for your selected product.

Do I need my Google review link?

No. For review products, send your Google business name and location and TapGrowth can identify the correct listing.

Are the monthly plans locked in?

No. TapGrowth monthly plans do not have a lock-in contract.

What is the difference between Smart Keychain and the $25 Landing Page plan?

The Smart Keychain is intentionally Lite: up to 3 core buttons and 1 change per month. The $25 plan is a fuller business landing page with a physical stand and up to 2 page changes per month.

Why are bookings and offers only on Pro?

Those higher-value features are reserved for Pro so each plan stays simple and clearly different.

Can Pro customers be featured on the TapGrowth website?

Yes. Pro customers can request optional featured-business placement during setup, subject to approval and availability.
