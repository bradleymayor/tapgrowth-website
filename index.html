Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before placing a TapGrowth order.

Do customers need an app?

No. Compatible smartphones can read the NFC stand directly and open the linked page.

Do I have to program the stand myself?

No. TapGrowth programs the stand to your business before it is sent.

Can I upload my business logo?

Yes. The order form lets you upload a JPG, JPEG or PNG logo/branding image. Landing Page + Stand and Pro customers can use their branding on the digital page, and Pro can include a custom co-branded stand concept.

What happens before I pay?

Choose your package and submit your business information first. After a successful submission, you are taken to a confirmation page with the secure Stripe checkout button for that plan.

Can my landing page be changed later?

Yes. The Landing Page + Stand plan includes up to 2 page changes per month. TapGrowth Pro includes unlimited page changes.

Is shipping included?

Yes. Physical TapGrowth stand packages include free shipping Australia-wide.

How long does setup and delivery take?

Timing depends on the customisation required. After your order details and payment are received, TapGrowth will contact you with the expected setup and dispatch timeframe.
