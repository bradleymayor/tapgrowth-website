Information

Common Questions.

Do customers need an app?

No. Compatible phones can read the NFC stand or keychain directly.

Do I need to program anything?

No. TapGrowth prepares the NFC destination for your selected product.

Are the monthly plans locked in?

No. TapGrowth monthly plans do not have a lock-in contract.

What is the difference between Smart Keychain and the $25 Landing Page plan?

The Smart Keychain is Lite: up to 3 core buttons and 1 change per month. The $25 plan is a fuller business landing page with a physical stand and up to 2 page changes per month.
