FAQ

Common Questions.

Do customers need an app?

No. Compatible phones can read the NFC stand directly.

Do I need to program the stand?

No. TapGrowth programs it before it is sent to you.

Do I need my Google review link?

No. For a Google Review Stand, just give us your business name as it appears on Google and its location. TapGrowth can find the correct listing.

What is included in the $25 monthly plan?

Your custom landing page can include Google Reviews, Website / Store, Instagram, Facebook, Directions and Contact. It also includes up to 2 page changes per month.

What does TapGrowth Pro add?

Pro adds Bookings, Offers / Menu / Products, analytics reporting, priority support, up to 5 page changes per month and the option to request featured business placement on TapGrowth.com.au.

Is a TapGrowth.com.au feature guaranteed to bring customers?

No. It is an optional promotional placement on the TapGrowth website, not a guarantee of traffic, leads or sales.

What happens after I submit my details?

You continue to the secure payment page for your selected package. TapGrowth then uses the submitted information to build and program your setup.
