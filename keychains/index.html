FAQ

Keychain Questions.

How much is standalone keychain shipping?

$10.95 per standalone keychain order. If a keychain is added to a main TapGrowth plan during that order, there is no extra keychain shipping charge.

What happens when someone taps the keychain?

Their compatible phone opens the destination programmed to the keychain.

Do customers need an app?

No. Compatible phones can read the NFC keychain directly.

Can the Smart Keychain have bookings or offers?

No. Those features are reserved for TapGrowth Pro.
