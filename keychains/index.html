FAQ

Keychain Questions.

What happens when someone taps the keychain?

Their compatible phone opens the destination programmed to the keychain — either your Google review page or Smart Keychain landing page.

Do customers need an app?

No. Compatible phones can read the NFC keychain directly.

Is the Smart Keychain locked into a contract?

No. The $15/month Smart Keychain plan has no lock-in contract.

Can the Smart Keychain have bookings or offers?

No. Those are kept for TapGrowth Pro so the Smart Keychain remains a simple Lite product.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. A Smart Keychain customer can move to a full Landing Page + Stand or TapGrowth Pro setup later.

I already pay for a TapGrowth landing page. Do I need another subscription?

No. Existing monthly customers can add a matching keychain for $15 one-time and have it open their current TapGrowth page.
