Privacy Policy

How TapGrowth handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you contact TapGrowth or start an order, you may provide your name, business name, email, phone number, business links, address, logo or branding files and other setup information.

How we use it

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, prepare and support TapGrowth products and services, fulfil orders, and communicate about your requested setup.

Forms and payments

Website form submissions are processed by a third-party form service. Payments are processed through Stripe. TapGrowth does not receive or store your full payment card details on this website.

Business files

Logos and other business assets you provide are used to create or support the TapGrowth setup you requested. Do not upload material you do not have permission to use.

Contact

If you have a privacy question or want information you submitted corrected, contact TapGrowth through the website contact form.
