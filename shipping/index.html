Shipping

Shipping information for physical TapGrowth stand orders in Australia.

Australia-wide shipping

Physical stand packages displayed on TapGrowth include free standard shipping within Australia unless otherwise stated at checkout.

Dispatch timing

TapGrowth products are programmed and may be customised before dispatch. Timing therefore depends on the information and assets required for your order. TapGrowth will communicate the expected setup and dispatch timeframe after your order details and payment are received.

Delivery details

You are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address. Delivery time after dispatch depends on the carrier and destination.

Delivery problems

If a parcel is delayed, damaged or appears lost, contact TapGrowth with your order details so the issue can be investigated.
